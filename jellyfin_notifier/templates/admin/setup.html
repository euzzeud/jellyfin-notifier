Danger zone

Resets absolutely everything back to a fresh, never-configured install: the .env file (all credentials — SMTP/admin passwords, API keys), settings.json (mail texts & colors, schedule, poller/Jellyfin/SMTP overrides), the pending mail queue, the "upcoming" titles list and their posters, the poller's "already seen" state, and the two HTML email templates (restored to what ships with this install — your edits are still kept as a timestamped backup). This cannot be undone. Since nothing would be configured afterwards, you'll be dropped straight back into this setup wizard.
